How hotel status works

Each chain runs a free loyalty program and sorts members into tiers by how much they stay. You start at the bottom when you join, move up as nights add up over a calendar year, and better tiers bring better treatment at the hotel.

The five programs at a glance

Outlined tier: where daily breakfast starts at UAE hotels. Nights count per calendar year; most programs also offer a stays, spend or points route, shown in the detailed guides.

How you move up

What counts

Paid nights booked through the chain count everywhere. Some programs also count stays, spend or base points, and award nights count at some chains but not others.

When it resets

Qualification runs on the calendar year. A tier you reach is usually kept through the following year, so status earned in 2026 carries you through 2027.

Shortcuts

Some UAE credit cards include status or elite night credits, and status matches can lift you a tier on the strength of another program.

Benefits that change with tier

Before you count on a benefit

  • Tier benefits usually apply only to bookings made on the chain's own site or app, not through travel agencies or booking sites.
  • Upgrades and late checkout depend on what the hotel has free on the day.
  • Some brands, resorts and partner hotels give less or nothing. The detailed guides list the exceptions that matter in the UAE.
